# Course Platform

The language of a platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Language

### Purchases

**Personal Purchase**:
A learner buying a course for themselves, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Individual purchase

**Team Purchase**:
Someone buying seats in a course for other people to claim. It never gives the buyer access, and how it is refunded is not yet decided.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, seat purchase

**Refund**:
Giving a learner back exactly the amount they paid for their Personal Purchase, at the learner's own request. A Refund is Requested, then Approved or Declined; an Approved Refund ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Refund Request

**Declined Refund**:
A Refund a team member refuses after review. The only reasons are a Chargeback on the same purchase, a learner abusing Refunds across courses, or a fraudulent purchase; any other Refundable purchase is Approved.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen days after a purchase during which the learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refundable**:
A Personal Purchase is Refundable when it is inside its Refund Window, the learner has watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons, and it has never had a Refund, whatever that Refund's outcome.

**Chargeback**:
A cardholder disputing a charge through their bank or card issuer. The platform neither starts it nor decides it, so it is never a Refund.
_Avoid_: Dispute (when you mean a Refund)
