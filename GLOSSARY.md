# Course Platform

The language of a platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Language

### Purchases

**Personal Purchase**:
A learner buying a course for themselves, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Individual purchase

**Team Purchase**:
Someone buying seats in a course for other people to claim. It never gives the buyer access, and how it is refunded is not yet decided.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, seat purchase

**Refund**:
Giving a learner back exactly the amount they paid for their Personal Purchase, at the learner's own request. A Refund is Requested, then an admin Approves or Declines it; an Approved Refund ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Refund Request

**Declined Refund**:
A Refund an admin refuses after review. The admin must give one of exactly three reasons: a Chargeback on the same purchase, Refund Abuse, or a Fraudulent Purchase. Any other Refund is Approved. Once Approved or Declined, a Refund's outcome is final.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Abuse**:
A learner using Refunds across several courses in a way an admin judges to be taking advantage of them. There is no fixed threshold; it is the admin's call.
_Avoid_: Serial refunding

**Fraudulent Purchase**:
A Personal Purchase an admin believes was made with a payment method the buyer was not entitled to use.

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen days after a purchase during which the learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refundable**:
A Personal Purchase is Refundable when it is inside its Refund Window, the learner has watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons, and it has never had a Refund, whatever that Refund's outcome. It is judged once, when the learner requests the Refund; it is not judged again when an admin reviews it, even if the Refund Window has since closed.

**Chargeback**:
A cardholder disputing a charge through their bank or card issuer. The platform neither starts it nor decides it, so it is never a Refund.
_Avoid_: Dispute (when you mean a Refund)
